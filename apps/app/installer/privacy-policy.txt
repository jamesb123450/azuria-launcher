AZURIA LAUNCHER — PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: 19 October 2026

ABOUT THIS POLICY
Azuria Launcher is an independent Minecraft Java launcher developed and maintained by Azuria Studios. This policy explains how personal information is used when you use the launcher. Azuria is not affiliated with Microsoft or Mojang.
For questions or privacy requests, visit https://launcher.azuriasmp.org/contact/. Do not include private information in public support issues.

INFORMATION AND HOW IT IS USED
Azuria stores your preferences, Minecraft profile, instances, downloaded content, skins, screenshots and game logs on your computer to provide the launcher’s features. Saved sign-in credentials are protected using Windows Credential Manager. Azuria does not receive or store your Microsoft password.
When you sign in, Microsoft, Xbox and Minecraft Services process your account details to authenticate you, verify game ownership and provide your profile, skins and capes.
Requests for updates, game files and community content include connection information, such as your IP address, and relevant searches, version or content identifiers. These requests are used to deliver updates, search results and downloads, and to protect the services against abuse.
If you contact the project, Azuria Studios receives the information you provide, such as your username, message and attachments, to respond and investigate the issue. Public GitHub issues can be viewed by others.
Azuria does not include launcher analytics or automatically upload your game logs to Azuria Studios. It does not sell personal information or include targeted advertising in the launcher.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
Azuria connects to Microsoft/Mojang, GitHub, selected mod-loader services, Modrinth, CurseForge and their download providers as needed. CurseForge catalogue requests may pass through an Azuria service hosted by Cloudflare. These providers process request information under their own privacy policies and may process it outside your country.
Optional Discord activity shares a generic Playing Minecraft status and start time with your local Discord client. It does not include your account, instance, world or server name. You can disable it in the launcher or Discord.
Provider privacy policies:
Microsoft: https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement
GitHub: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
Modrinth: https://modrinth.com/legal/privacy
CurseForge: https://www.curseforge.com/legal/privacy-policy
Cloudflare: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/
Discord: https://discord.com/privacy

RETENTION AND YOUR CHOICES
Local files remain on your computer until you remove them. Signing out removes Azuria’s saved sign-in credentials. Uninstalling the launcher does not automatically delete instances, worlds, screenshots, logs, local trash or backups.
Support information is retained while needed to resolve enquiries, maintain the project or meet legal obligations. Public issue history may remain available through GitHub. Other providers set their own retention periods.
Privacy settings let you control account reconnection, visible account details and optional activity sharing. The installer saves a local acknowledgement after first installation. Automatic updates do not request acceptance again. This acknowledgement is not consent to advertising, analytics or unrelated data collection.

YOUR RIGHTS
Where applicable, processing to respond to support requests and protect Azuria’s services is based on legitimate interests in supporting users and maintaining secure services. Other providers explain their own legal bases in their policies.
Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of personal information held by the project, or object to its processing. Use the contact page to arrange a suitable private route. Requests relating to Microsoft or another provider should be directed to that provider. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in the UK, visit https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/.

POLICY UPDATES
The latest policy is available at https://launcher.azuriasmp.org/privacy/. Material changes to how Azuria uses personal information will be communicated before the new processing starts. Website hosting and any website advertising are described separately on that page. Terms of service are available at https://launcher.azuriasmp.org/terms/.
