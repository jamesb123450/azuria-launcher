AZURIA LAUNCHER — PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: 18 October 2026

ABOUT AZURIA
Azuria Launcher is an independent Minecraft Java launcher maintained by jamesb123450 on GitHub. It is not operated by Microsoft or Mojang.
Contact and privacy requests: https://launcher.azuriasmp.org/contact/
Public support: https://github.com/jamesb123450/azuria-releases/issues
Do not post passwords, tokens or private personal information in public issues.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR COMPUTER
Azuria stores instance configurations, launcher preferences, account profile information, skin-library files, screenshots, downloads and game logs locally. Active instances and launcher data are stored in %APPDATA%\AzuriaLauncher. Saved Microsoft refresh credentials use Windows Credential Manager; authentication tokens are handled by the native launcher rather than exposed to its web interface.
Azuria does not include analytics collection or automatically upload your logs to the maintainer.

MICROSOFT AND MINECRAFT
Choosing sign-in connects to Microsoft, Xbox and Minecraft Services to authenticate your account, check Java Edition access and retrieve your profile, skins and capes. Your default browser handles Microsoft sign-in; Azuria does not ask you to enter your Microsoft password into its own form. These services receive the information required to perform those requests.
Microsoft privacy statement: https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement

UPDATES, GAME FILES AND CONTENT
Azuria checks GitHub for launcher updates and downloads signed packages. Game preparation and version selectors contact Mojang/Microsoft and the selected loader metadata/download services. Discover, content details and downloads contact Modrinth or CurseForge and approved download hosts. Providers receive normal request information, including your IP address, and the requested search terms, versions or content identifiers.
Configured CurseForge builds use an Azuria Cloudflare-hosted service to forward catalogue and project/file metadata requests. The publisher's API credential remains on that service. It receives no Microsoft tokens, game logs or account identifiers. Cloudflare processes ordinary request metadata and applies rate limits. Content files download from approved provider hosts; author download restrictions are respected.
Provider privacy information:
https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
https://modrinth.com/legal/privacy
https://www.curseforge.com/legal/privacy-policy
https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

OPTIONAL DISCORD ACTIVITY
Configured builds can send generic Playing Minecraft activity and its start time to your local Discord client while Minecraft runs. This does not include your account, instance, world or server names. It does not require a Discord sign-in inside Azuria. Control this in Settings > Privacy or Behaviour, or through Discord's own activity controls.
Discord privacy policy: https://discord.com/privacy

YOUR CHOICES AND RETENTION
Settings > Privacy controls automatic account reconnection and hiding your account name/skin head in the launcher. Hiding details does not hide them inside Minecraft or the skin editor.
Sign out in Profile to remove Azuria's saved Microsoft credentials. Local preferences, game files, screenshots and logs remain until you remove them. Removed instances/files may be retained in local trash; replaced content may be retained in instance backups. Uninstalling the launcher does not automatically erase these retained files. Back up any worlds or files you want to keep before deleting local data.

SUPPORT AND YOUR RIGHTS
If you choose to submit a GitHub issue, the maintainer receives your GitHub username and the information you include. Issues and their history are public and may remain available through GitHub; copies may exist outside the project's control. Support information is used to respond to requests, investigate problems and maintain Azuria.
Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict personal information, or object to processing. For information held by a provider, use that provider's privacy process; provider retention and international processing are described in its own policy. For information shared with the maintainer, use the contact page to arrange a suitable private route. UK users can contact the Information Commissioner's Office at https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/.

INSTALLER ACKNOWLEDGEMENT
The acceptance checkbox acknowledges this policy. It does not turn on optional features, subscribe you to marketing or authorise additional tracking. The installer records only this policy's version fingerprint in your Windows user registry after a successful installation. A changed policy requires a new acknowledgement; cancelling setup does not record it. Updates can reuse a previously accepted, unchanged policy. A silent install without acceptance stops instead of assuming agreement.
